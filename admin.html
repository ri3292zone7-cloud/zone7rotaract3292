🎪 District Events · RY 2026-27

Hosting a District Event? It Starts With a Bid.

Twice a Rotary year, Rotaract District 3292 hands one of its big signature events to a member club to organise: the 1st President–Secretary Meet and the President Night. Hosting isn’t an assignment — it’s a competitive bid: any club (or co-host clubs) can apply, and the Rotaract District Committee (RDC) picks the strongest proposal. The event budget, logistics, food, venue and hospitality are the host club’s job; the RDC runs the agenda, speakers and official programme.

1
Read the documentUnderstand exactly what the event demands before you commit.
2
Get club approvalPass the hosting decision in a general meeting and keep signed minutes.
3
Build your bidEOI letter + completed bidding form + venue proposal with photos & a location map.
4
Submit in timeSend the full bid as a PDF to both district emails before 11:59 PM on the deadline.
5
Present & winBidders may present to the RDC; the best plan gets the hosting rights.
Event 1 · RY 2026-27

1st President–Secretary Meet

Rotaract 3292’s formal annual summit of club Presidents and Secretaries — resolutions, updates and leadership, all in one day.

📅 Proposed date21 November 2026
⏱ Duration1 day · 8:00 AM – 8:00 PM
👥 Expected turnoutApprox. 500–800 participants
🏢 Venue neededTwo air-conditioned halls (800 + 300–400 seats)

What is it, in plain words

The 1st PS Meet is the district’s official get-together for the people steering every club — the President and Secretary of each Rotaract Club in District 3292. One of two PS Meets held every Rotary year, this edition sits in the first half of the year. Clubs and the district bring their key issues forward, everything is deliberated openly, and resolutions are reached together — so every club ends the meet knowing the district is moving in one direction. It’s also a rare chance for leaders to swap ideas and build networks across clubs.

How the one day breaks down

🎤 Formal MeetingRun strictly by the RDC and chaired by the DRR, with the District Secretary facilitating. Pre-circulated agendas are deliberated. Only Presidents, Secretaries (or an authorised rep with a signed consent letter on club letterhead) take part.
🎓 General Session (parallel)Runs at the same time in the second hall: results and capacity-building sessions by experienced resource persons — the Rotary–Rotaract framework, policy updates, grants and leadership.
🤝 Fellowship & InteractionThe second half goes informal — networking, collaboration and fun, designed together by the RDC and the host club.

What your club must arrange (the logistics checklist)

Two fully air-conditioned halls (Hall 1 ≈800, Hall 2 ≈300–400, theatre seating)
Stage setup: podiums with desktop mics, and a dais for 6–8 officials
At least 2 volunteers at the entrance for delegate verification
Branding: banners, selfie standee, checkerboard backdrop, photo stand & welcome gate
Hall 1: LED display screen with a dedicated technician
Hall 2: projector + HDMI/Type-C cables, presenter pointer and power
Sound: 4–6 wireless + 2 desk mics (Hall 1); 2 wireless + 1 desk mic (Hall 2)
Lighting and continuous, uninterrupted power all day
Professional photographer / videographer for full coverage
Delegate kit: ID card + lanyard, schedule, food coupons, diary & pen, kit bag
Catering: breakfast, lunch, hi-tea and dinner (non-veg included at least once)
On-theme stage and venue decoration (balloons, floral, official theme)

  • Venue proposal with photographs and a location map, plus the venue’s authorization letter
  • Contact person details for coordination, and a written bid in PDF format

Event 2 · RY 2026-27

President Night

The district’s two-day leadership retreat under the stars — bonding, team games and honest reflection for presidents and the RDC.

📅 Proposed dates25–26 September 2026
⏱ Duration1 night · 2 days
👥 Expected turnoutApprox. 250–300 participants
🌿 Venue vibeA safe, eco-friendly resort, hotel or campsite

What is it, in plain words

Away from the formality of meetings, President Night is the district’s flagship fellowship retreat for all club Presidents together with the Rotaract District Committee. The goal is simple, though it may be the part of leadership nobody else teaches: relaxed time to bond, laugh and recharge, so the people leading clubs actually trust each other. Expect icebreakers, team games, a cultural fellowship evening around the campfire — then a calm morning activity and a leadership reflection session before everyone heads home.

Day-by-day breakdown

🌤 Day 1 — Arrival & funArrival and registration, opening ceremony, icebreakers, team-building games, then the President Night cultural fellowship, followed by overnight stay.
🌅 Day 2 — Reflect & closeEarly-morning outdoor activity, a leadership reflection session, breakfast, closing ceremony and departure.
🍛 Food planFive meals in total — Day 1: hi-tea, fellowship snacks and dinner; Day 2: breakfast and lunch. Non-veg included at least once, drinking water always available.

What your club must arrange

A safe, clean, eco-friendly resort, hotel or campsite
A hall for 250–300 people (theater-style or round tables)
Spaces for both indoor sessions and outdoor games
Registration & welcome desk on arrival
Campfire or evening gathering space
Power backup and uninterrupted lighting
Branding: banners, selfie standee, backdrop, photo stand & welcome gate
LED display screen with a technician, plus a sound system (2 wireless + 2 desk mics)
Professional photographer / videographer
Overnight shared accommodation plus all 5 meals & safe drinking water
Delegate kit: ID card + lanyard, schedule, notebook & pen, badge or ribbon
Theme-consistent stage and venue decoration

Ground rules that apply to every bid

📜 Venue permission letterEvery bid must include an authorization letter from the venue confirming it is available and cleared for the event.
🤝 You own your promisesThe bidding club is individually responsible for every commitment made in its proposal.
🚫 No silent changesVenue, date, schedule, budget, food, speakers or fees cannot change without prior written RDC approval.
🛡 Safe & suitable venueThe venue must be safe, hygienic and accessible; the RDC can inspect it or ask for evidence before final approval.
🍽 Hospitality standardsFood must be hygienic, meals served on time, and dietary requirements accommodated where feasible.
⚠️ Non-compliance costsWarnings, restriction or disqualification from hosting future district events follow until matters are fixed.
