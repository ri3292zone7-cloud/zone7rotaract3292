RI & District Dues Calculator (RY 2026-27 · collection opens 1 July)

Rotary International charges $0/member for your club type. Rotaract District 3292 charges a flat NRs 200/member. The NPR figure for RI dues is an estimate, since banks apply their own conversion rate on the day you actually pay. Both are due before the district's payment deadline.
