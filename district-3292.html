Rotaract District 3292 Nepal Bhutan

Rotaract District 3292 Nepal Bhutan is the Rotary district for Nepal and Bhutan. Zone 7 Rotaract is its Kathmandu Valley zone — 9 Rotaract clubs: Balkumari, Baneshwor, Liberty College, Kathmandu West, Kathmandu Height, Sankhu, New Road City Kathmandu, Sukedhara and Tripureswor. What is Rotaract?

Rotaract is Rotary International's service club for young adults 18–30. Members run their own clubs, lead service projects, and build fellowship and leadership skills. Zone 7 Rotaract is part of Rotaract District 3292 Nepal Bhutan.

Zone 7 in District 3292

District 3292 covers Nepal and Bhutan and is divided into zones. Zone 7 covers the Kathmandu Valley. The Zonal Rotaract Representative (ZRR) for RY 2026-27 is Rajay Bajracharya (Sukedhara). Zone 7 clubs meet weekly, run blood donation camps, health camps, and education projects, and compete for the barometer.

How to join Rotaract in Nepal

  1. Go to zone7rotaract3292.vercel.app/join — fill the 1-min form (18–30, no fees to apply)
  2. Pick a Zone 7 club or choose “help me match” — we place you near Kathmandu Valley
  3. Attend a meeting as a guest, then become a Rotaractor
