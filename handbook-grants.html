Handbook · Chapter 01

Turn a good idea into funded service.

Rotary funding comes in two speeds. District Grants are small, local and fast; they support short-term projects that answer an urgent community need. Global Grants are large, international and rigorous; they fund sustainable projects aligned with the 7 Areas of Focus. This chapter walks both paths end to end, with the rules that keep the money honest.

Rules per the District Fund & Grant Criterion Document and the directory's Global Grant guide. Amounts, matching percentages and deadlines that change every year (set by the District Governor) are noted as such. Check the current directory before applying.
