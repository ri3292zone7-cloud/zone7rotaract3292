Who we are

Service above self, led by youth.

Rotaract is Rotary International's club for young adults aged 18–30, sponsored by local Rotary clubs but entirely run by Rotaractors themselves — from what projects to take on, to how the club is organized.

Rotaract District 3292 covers Nepal and Bhutan, and is one of the most active Rotaract movements in the region — with a history spanning more than three decades and a membership of over 5,500 young leaders across 180+ clubs.

Zone 7 is one of the district's zones, made up of Rotaract clubs based in and around the Kathmandu Valley. Guided each Rotary year by a Zone Rotaract Representative (ZRR), Zone 7 clubs meet, collaborate on joint service projects, and support each other's growth — acting as a bridge between individual clubs and the wider District 3292 leadership.

Together, Zone 7 clubs run service projects in education, health, environment and community development, while giving members real leadership and professional-development experience along the way.
