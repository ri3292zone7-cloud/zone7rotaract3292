Try It First

Show up before you sign up.

Every Zone 7 club runs on the standard Rotaract rhythm of meetings and fellowship. You're welcome as a guest at any of it. No form required, no commitment.

  • Regular meetingsthe club's heartbeat. Clubs aim for 24 a year, guests welcome any week
  • Board meetingsrun by each club's elected officers. The monthly planning engine
  • 2 club assembliesa year, where members vote on the club's direction
  • Fellowshipshikes, picnics, festival nights and birthday celebrations

Where You'll Fit

Every member serves through a committee.

New members join one of five committees, each with its own goals and projects for the year. Pick based on what you actually enjoy.

🎉

Club Service

Keeps the club alive: meetings, orientation, fellowship events, installations and membership growth.

 🩸

Community Service

The projects people see: blood drives, health camps, school support, environment and awareness campaigns.

 🌍

International Service

Twinship, letterhead exchanges, youth exchanges and joint events with clubs abroad.

 🎓

Professional Development

Skills that outlast Rotaract: public speaking, resume clinics, first-aid training, video editing, podcasting.

 💰

Finance

Budgets, dues, fundraising and transparent money management, the club's trust backbone.
