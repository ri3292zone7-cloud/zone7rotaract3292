Plan a camp that saves lives.

One of the most visible service projects a club runs, and one of the most regulated. Plan the logistics, let the blood bank run the medicine, and every unit counts.

The legal and safety framework follows the licensed blood bank rules that govern camps in Nepal, with eligibility from the WHO Blood Donor Selection Guidelines and the Nepal Red Cross blood service, and the District 3292 district fund available to cover costs. Source documents are listed under each section below.

Inside this tutorial

The legal and medical basics first, then the partnership, eligibility, planning, the day-of flow, first aid and the wrap-up. Each section ends with key points and its source documents.

Watch it in action

Three donation drives end to end, from registration and screening to collection and donor care.
