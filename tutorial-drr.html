District · The Bigger Picture

The district comes to your club.

The District Rotaract Representative, or DRR, leads Rotaract across the whole district on behalf of the District Governor. A DRR visit is bigger than a ZRR visit: it is recognition, coaching and a direct line to the district's resources and leadership. This tutorial covers who the DRR is, what a visit really means for your club, and how to host one that leaves everyone better, including the DRR.

Based on the District 3292 Rotaract Guidebook, the Standard Rotaract Club Constitution and Rotary International's Rotaract guidance. Your club's own constitution and bylaws always win when they differ.
