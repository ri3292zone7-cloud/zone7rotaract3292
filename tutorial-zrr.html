Zone · The Bridge

The zone's bridge to your club.

The Zonal Rotaract Representative, or ZRR, is the link between your club and the district: a trained Rotaractor who knows the zone's clubs, visits them regularly, monitors their health and connects them to district programs. Every Zone 7 club should host at least two ZRR visits a year. This tutorial covers who the ZRR is, why those visits matter, and exactly how to host one that strengthens your club instead of just filling an hour.

Based on the District 3292 Rotaract Guidebook and the Standard Rotaract Club Constitution. The exact visit requirements come from the district's annual plan.
